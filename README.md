## Mechanical engineering, building energy and practical tools

I’m Pierre-Luc Paradis, PhD, PE, a Senior Design Engineer at Ecosystem and a licensed Professional Engineer in Vermont and Maryland. I work on energy efficiency, HVAC, heat-transfer and refrigeration systems for institutional and commercial buildings, primarily across Québec, Ontario and the northeastern United States.

I grew up on a dairy farm in Mont-Carmel, Québec, near Kamouraska. I studied mechanical engineering in Rimouski before completing a master’s degree and PhD at École de technologie supérieure in Montréal. My graduate work focused on solar energy, heat transfer and transcritical CO₂ heat-pump systems.

I now live in rural Vermont. My niche is bridging hands-on mechanical design with digital operations: building and optimizing energy systems, then creating the data pipelines and practical Python tools needed to verify performance and make the work repeatable.

### Selected engineering work

- Campus chilled-water heat recovery and winter heat-rejection reduction
- Central steam-plant controls, master sequencing and economizer optimization
- Long-term measurement and verification using building-management-system data
- Weather and climate-data tools supporting energy design and operations
- Design and commissioning of a transcritical CO₂ heat pump coupled to a hybrid photovoltaic/thermal solar collector

### Current interests

- High-performance HVAC and heat-recovery systems
- Building energy analysis and operational optimization
- Heat pumps, refrigeration and thermal storage
- Measurement, verification and data-quality workflows
- Python, automation and open-source engineering tools
